Mechanistic interpretability · measurement report

Seeing is not steering

A linear probe reads the residual stream; a steering vector writes to it. Those two operations are governed by different subspaces, and nothing in training ties them together. This report derives the gap, proves it can be made total, and measures it — in constructed systems, in transformers trained from scratch, and in three pretrained language models on real toxicity data.
